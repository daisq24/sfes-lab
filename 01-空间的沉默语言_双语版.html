缄默的空间会梦见机器人吗？

Does Silent Space Dream of Robots? Machine Perception, the Built Environment,
and the Incommensurability of Human Civilization
空间的沉默语言 · 机器感知、建成环境与人类文明的不可通约性
Author · 戴诗琦 DAI Shiqi  |  M.Arch & Urban Studies, Tsinghua University  |  Keywords: AGI · Environmental Psychology · Embodied Cognition · Collective Memory · Material Civilization
摘要 · Abstract
人工通用智能（AGI）由语言至真实世界的研究正日趋热烈，然而学界对其认知边界的反思往往停留于语言与逻辑层面，未从人类文明于实体落地的维度——物质空间与建成环境及其对意识本身的反向塑造——进行考量。建筑与城市作为集体记忆的物质载体，凝结着情感、权力、苦难与创造力的历史积淀（Halbwachs, 1992; Nora, 1989）。本文从环境心理学、具身认知与文化记忆理论出发，探讨当前机器感知范式在理解人类空间智能方面的根本局限，并呼吁将情感、文化与人文维度纳入 AGI 训练目标。
Keywords · 关键词
Research carrying Artificial General Intelligence from language toward the real world is intensifying; yet reflection on its cognitive limits tends to halt at the levels of language and logic, neglecting the dimension in which human civilization is materially grounded—physical space and the built environment, and their reciprocal shaping of consciousness itself. Architecture and cities, as material carriers of collective memory, condense a historical sediment of emotion, power, suffering, and creativity (Halbwachs, 1992; Nora, 1989). Drawing on environmental psychology, embodied cognition, and cultural memory theory, this paper examines the fundamental limits of current machine-perception paradigms and calls for incorporating emotional, cultural, and humanistic dimensions into AGI's training objectives.

I.
建成环境：人类智能的物质结晶
The Built Environment: The Material Crystallization of Human Intelligence
建成环境（Built Environment）不仅是功能性庇护所，更是文化逻辑、权力秩序与集体渴望的物质编码——是人类智能的外化形式之一。
The built environment is not merely functional shelter but a material encoding of cultural logic, the order of power, and collective longing—one of the externalized forms of human intelligence.
建筑是人类创造的最古老也最持久的艺术形式之一，它不仅满足了人类的居住、工作、娱乐等生活需要，也反映了人类的文化、思想、审美和技术的发展。
Architecture is one of the oldest and most enduring art forms humanity has created; it not only meets our needs for dwelling, work, and leisure, but also reflects the development of human culture, thought, aesthetics, and technology.
城市与建筑——从紫禁城的轴线布局到巴黎圣母院的哥特尖顶，从西非约鲁巴的庭院住宅到中国徽州的马头墙——在反映动态语言与社会结构之外，可被概括为一套套因地因时、凝练而持久的文明的营造语法。
Cities and architecture—from the axial layout of the Forbidden City to the Gothic spires of Notre-Dame, from the courtyard houses of the Yoruba in West Africa to the horse-head gable walls of Huizhou—beyond reflecting dynamic language and social structure, can be read as so many grammars of civilizational making: place- and time-specific, compressed, and enduring.
比较经典的理论大致如下：Rapoport（1969）在其奠基性研究《住屋形式与文化》中指出，住屋形式并非纯粹的功能性回应，而是文化价值、社会结构与宇宙观的物质表达；门楣上的雕花、天井的朝向乃至屋脊的曲线，皆是文化逻辑的凝练编码，承载着代代相传的审美意志与生存哲学。Lefebvre（1991）进一步将空间理论化为社会关系的产物与容器：空间既是权力运作的场域，亦是日常生活抵抗与创造的舞台——《空间的生产》一书提示我们，物质空间从不中立。Lynch（1960）则揭示了城市意象——路径、边界、区域、节点与标志物——如何在居民的认知地图中构建出意义的坐标系，为城市居民提供存在于世的方向感与归属感。
The classic theories run roughly as follows. Rapoport (1969), in his foundational House Form and Culture, argued that dwelling forms are not purely functional responses but material expressions of cultural values, social structure, and cosmology: the carved lintel, the orientation of the courtyard, even the curve of a ridgeline are dense encodings of cultural logic, carrying aesthetic will and a philosophy of survival across generations. Lefebvre (1991) further theorized space as both product and container of social relations—space is at once a field of power and a stage for the resistance and creativity of everyday life; The Production of Space reminds us that material space is never neutral. Lynch (1960) revealed how urban imageability—paths, edges, districts, nodes, and landmarks—constructs a coordinate system of meaning within residents' cognitive maps, giving city-dwellers a sense of orientation and belonging in the world.
这些理论共同指向一个核心命题：建筑与城市是人类文明的艺术，是智能的结晶，也是最难以被硅基生命们"读懂"的具象文本。
These theories converge on a central proposition: architecture and cities are the art of human civilization, the crystallization of intelligence, and the concrete text most resistant to being "read" by silicon-based life.

II.
具身性与空间理解
Ontological — Embodiment and Spatial Understanding
当代深度学习在图像识别、空间导航乃至三维重建方面已取得显著成就（LeCun et al., 2015），然而深度学习算法的数学本质可溯源于概率及模式匹配，而非意义理解。Dreyfus（1992）早在人工智能的早期发展阶段便指出，计算机的根本局限在于缺乏"具身的情境性"（embodied situatedness）——即通过身体在世界中的持续存在而获得的理解能力。机器人现在可以作诗，但它无法理解我们的空间——这不是算法的缺陷，而是认知范式的根本差异。
Contemporary deep learning has achieved remarkable results in image recognition, spatial navigation, and even three-dimensional reconstruction (LeCun et al., 2015); yet the mathematical essence of these algorithms traces back to probability and pattern-matching, not to the understanding of meaning. Dreyfus (1992) noted, in the earliest days of AI, that the computer's fundamental limitation is the absence of "embodied situatedness"—the understanding that arises from a body's sustained presence in the world. Robots can now compose poetry; they cannot understand our spaces. This is not an algorithmic defect but a fundamental difference of cognitive paradigm.
感知不是一种被动的信息接收，而是身体主动与世界接触、协商与建构意义的过程。
Perception is not the passive reception of information, but the body's active engagement with the world—a process of contact, negotiation, and the construction of meaning.
— Merleau-Ponty, Phenomenology of Perception, 1962
Merleau-Ponty（1962）的现象学为此提供了哲学基础：感知不是一种被动的信息接收，而是身体主动与世界接触、协商与建构意义的过程。人在进入一座老宅时感受到的"重量"——木料的气味、脚踏地板的微微形变、光线从天井斜切而入的角度——这种综合性的身体体验构成了空间意义的基础，是任何摄像头与传感器阵列都无法复现的现象学经验。基于 Transformer 架构的大语言模型，可被概括为 Held & Hein（1963）的"小猫旋转木马实验"（Kitten Carousel Experiment）中那只"看到了"世界但"并未学会去看"的小猫。Varela 等人（1991）将此发展为"具身认知"（enaction）理论，强调认知是生命体在世界中行动的涌现属性，而非在大脑中处理表征的计算过程。
Merleau-Ponty's (1962) phenomenology grounds this philosophically: perception is not the passive reception of information but the body's active contact with the world, negotiating and constructing meaning. The "weight" one feels on entering an old house—the scent of timber, the faint give of the floorboards underfoot, the angle at which light slants in through the courtyard—this integrated bodily experience constitutes the foundation of spatial meaning, a phenomenological encounter no camera or sensor array can reproduce. A large language model built on the Transformer architecture may be likened to the kitten in Held and Hein's (1963) "kitten carousel" experiment: the one that "saw" the world yet never learned to see. Varela, Thompson, and Rosch (1991) developed this into the theory of "enaction," emphasizing that cognition is an emergent property of a living being acting in the world, not a computation that manipulates representations in the brain.
Gibson（1979）的"可供性"（affordance）理论亦揭示了同样的裂隙：空间的意义并非存在于客观物理属性中，而是在有机体与环境的关系性互动中生成的。一道低矮的门楣，对高大者是障碍，对儿童是游戏的邀请；一片广场的空旷，对追求认可的集会者是舞台，对逃避的个体则是暴露的危险。这种关系性的、情境嵌入的意义，是当前语言模型与视觉模型所无从捕捉的。
Gibson's (1979) theory of "affordances" reveals the same fissure: the meaning of space does not reside in objective physical properties but is generated in the relational interaction between organism and environment. A low lintel is an obstacle to the tall and an invitation to play for a child; the openness of a plaza is a stage for those who seek recognition and an exposed danger for those who wish to hide. This relational, situation-embedded meaning is something current language and vision models cannot capture.
Lakoff 与 Johnson（1999）在其具身心灵哲学中进一步论证，人类的抽象概念本身根植于身体经验：我们用"重量"描述责任，用"高度"描述地位，用"温度"描述情感——通感隐喻所反映的认知基础结构早已嵌入语言的方方面面，仅以概率的方式进行预测无疑是令具备真实觉知的生命感到困惑的。缺乏身体的机器，其对空间语言的"理解"犹如盲人学习色彩词汇：红的物理、红的社会含义、红在愤怒与喜庆里的双重身份均可基于语言产生关联理解与记忆，但并无法具备真实色彩交互所形成的感质（qualia：经验的主观或定性属性）。
Lakoff and Johnson (1999) argue further, in their philosophy of the embodied mind, that human abstract concepts are themselves rooted in bodily experience: we describe responsibility as "weight," status as "height," emotion as "temperature." The synesthetic-metaphorical structures that underlie cognition are long embedded in every aspect of language, and to predict them in a merely probabilistic way is bewildering to any being possessed of genuine awareness. A machine without a body "understands" spatial language as a blind person learns color words: the physics of red, the social meanings of red, red's double identity in anger and in celebration can all be associated, understood, and remembered through language—yet such a machine cannot possess the qualia (the subjective or qualitative properties of experience) that arise only from real chromatic interaction.
若以动物行为学的视角观察 AI 的发展历程与初衷，广义的"智能"当然应当于特定环境中通过感知、认知及行动间的动态交互而涌现。近年来，围绕 world models、embodied agents 与 active inference 的研究，正在尝试将智能重新置于"行动—环境—反馈"的闭环之中：智能体不再仅通过 token prediction 学习世界，而是借由与环境的持续交互形成动态空间表征、行动策略与长期记忆结构。类似 multimodal latent planning、environment-conditioned reasoning 与 self-supervised interaction 的研究路径，也正试图突破传统语言模型缺乏时空连续性的局限。因此，Theory of Space（Zhang et al., 2026）的工作格外珍贵地反映了人工智能领域在本体论（ontological）层面的本质性思考与问题界定层面的突破。
Viewed through the lens of ethology, the trajectory and original aspiration of AI suggest that "intelligence" in the broad sense ought to emerge through the dynamic interaction of perception, cognition, and action within a specific environment. In recent years, research around world models, embodied agents, and active inference has been working to place intelligence back within an "action–environment–feedback" loop: an agent no longer learns the world through token prediction alone, but forms dynamic spatial representations, action policies, and long-term memory structures through sustained interaction with its environment. Research paths such as multimodal latent planning, environment-conditioned reasoning, and self-supervised interaction are likewise attempting to break past the traditional language model's lack of spatio-temporal continuity. In this light, the Theory of Space work (Zhang et al., 2026) is an especially valuable reflection of a breakthrough, at the ontological level, in the essential thinking and problem-definition of the field of artificial intelligence.

III.
场所认同与集体记忆
Epistemological — Place Identity and Collective Memory
关键概念 · Key Concept
场所认同（Place-identity）：物理环境是自我概念的重要组成部分，人们通过对特定场所的依恋建构身份与归属感（Proshansky et al., 1983）。
Place-identity: the physical environment is an important component of the self-concept; people construct identity and belonging through attachment to specific places.
建成环境不仅是文化逻辑的编码，更是集体记忆的物质锚点。Halbwachs（1992）奠定了集体记忆理论的基础，指出记忆是社会性建构的，群体通过共享的物质环境来维持和传递其历史意识。Nora（1989）则提出"记忆之场"（lieux de mémoire）的概念：那些被赋予象征意义的地点——战场、纪念碑、历史街区——是群体记忆与国家认同的物质支撑，在被一次次后世地阅读与再创作中被赋予迭代累积而永不逆向的价值与意义。
The built environment is not only an encoding of cultural logic but a material anchor of collective memory. Halbwachs (1992) laid the foundations of collective-memory theory, arguing that memory is socially constructed and that groups sustain and transmit their historical consciousness through shared physical environments. Nora (1989) proposed the concept of lieux de mémoire—sites of memory: symbolically charged places such as battlefields, monuments, and historic quarters that serve as the material support of group memory and national identity, endowed—through being read and re-created again and again by later generations—with an accreting, irreversible value and meaning.
Proshansky 等人（1983）将此延伸为"场所认同"理论，认为物理环境是自我概念的重要组成部分：人们通过对特定场所的依恋与认同，建构其身份与归属感。Altman 与 Low（1992）的研究亦表明，场所依附（place attachment）是跨文化的普遍心理现象，然而其具体形式深刻地受到文化、历史与个体经历的塑造。
Proshansky et al. (1983) extended this into the theory of "place-identity," holding that the physical environment is an important component of the self-concept: people construct identity and belonging through attachment to, and identification with, particular places. Altman and Low (1992) likewise show that place attachment is a cross-culturally universal psychological phenomenon, even as its concrete forms are profoundly shaped by culture, history, and individual experience.
从华盛顿远道而来的乔治城伙伴们对北京的印象，总是源于紫禁城——这座帝制权威的空间化仪式。触摸到汉白玉台阶的一瞬，仿佛便能感知到在威严与恐惧中被编织进去的无数英雄豪杰、匠人宫人的生命；这座汉族与满族文化交汇的物质证据，在当代又成为中国人在民族记忆中不断重新诠释的象征资本；被威严与杀戮日夜更迭浇灌着的空间，凝结着可怜的渺小人类为了个人欲望的膨胀和权力争夺，用多少万同胞的尸骨与血泪堆砌而成的"美"。类似地，巴黎圣母院当然远不只是一座教堂——它是中世纪神学宇宙观的石材表达，是雨果笔下受苦灵魂的庇护所，于是当 2019 年那场烈火燃起，数亿人同时感受到文明脆弱性的震颤——因此要不惜一切代价地修补与挽回——这显然是一件无法被理性的金钱计算所衡量的事情，如若交由用于量化金融的模型进行估算，大抵 CBA（成本收益分析模型）是过不了批的。
For my Georgetown companions, who came all the way from Washington, their impression of Beijing always began with the Forbidden City—the spatialized ritual of imperial authority. In the instant of touching its white-marble steps, one seems to sense the lives of countless heroes, craftsmen, and palace servants woven into it amid majesty and fear; this material evidence of the meeting of Han and Manchu cultures has become, in our own time, symbolic capital that the Chinese continually reinterpret within national memory. A space irrigated day and night by alternating majesty and slaughter, it condenses a "beauty" piled up from the bones, blood, and tears of hundreds of thousands of fellow human beings—raised by pitiable, diminutive people in the swelling of personal desire and the contest for power. Likewise, Notre-Dame de Paris is of course far more than a church—it is the stone expression of a medieval theological cosmology, the shelter for suffering souls in Hugo's pages; so when the great fire broke out in 2019, hundreds of millions felt at once the tremor of civilization's fragility—and resolved to repair and reclaim it at any cost. This is plainly something that cannot be measured by rational monetary calculation: handed to a model of the kind used in quantitative finance, a cost-benefit analysis (CBA) would almost certainly fail to win approval.
类似 memory architectures 与 synthetic experience generation 的研究，则正在尝试赋予智能体跨时间尺度积累经验的能力：机器不再只依赖静态数据集，而是通过持续生成、回放与重组交互经验形成动态记忆结构。但问题或许正在于——记忆并不只是信息留存。Halbwachs 所强调的集体记忆，本质上依赖共同体中的情感确认、历史创伤与代际传递；而 Nora 所谓"记忆之场"的意义，也并不来自空间本身，而来自群体持续不断的再解释与再神圣化。机器或许能够形成长期空间表征，却仍未必能够进入"历史为何重要"这一价值维度。
Research on memory architectures and synthetic experience generation, in turn, is attempting to give agents the capacity to accumulate experience across timescales: the machine no longer relies on static datasets alone but forms dynamic memory structures by continuously generating, replaying, and recombining interactive experience. Yet the difficulty may lie precisely here—memory is not merely the retention of information. The collective memory Halbwachs emphasized depends, in essence, on emotional affirmation within a community, on historical trauma, and on intergenerational transmission; and the meaning of Nora's "sites of memory" comes not from space itself but from a group's ceaseless reinterpretation and re-sanctification. A machine may be able to form long-term spatial representations, yet it may still be unable to enter the dimension of value that asks why history matters.
当前机器视觉系统仍无从体验生物群落历时性且具备情感负载的空间意义。Bender 等人（2021）已明确指出大型语言模型本质上擅长统计性地复现训练数据中的语言模式，却缺乏对所指涉现实的真正理解。Marcus 与 Davis（2019）亦指出，当前 AI 系统严重缺乏常识推理与文化情境理解能力。尽管近期已有如 UrbanFeel（He et al., 2025）与 SocialNav（Guo et al., 2025）等工作试图从数据集与 Agent 架构集成方式将社会语义作为空间感知与导航能力的加成，但仍普遍停留于语义标注与显式匹配规则的浅层阶段，尚未实现社会语义与空间感知的深度耦合、动态演化与可解释性推理。
Current machine-vision systems still cannot experience the diachronic, emotionally loaded spatial meaning of living communities. Bender et al. (2021) have made clear that large language models are essentially adept at statistically reproducing the linguistic patterns in their training data while lacking any genuine understanding of the realities those patterns denote. Marcus and Davis (2019) likewise note that current AI systems are severely deficient in commonsense reasoning and cultural-contextual understanding. Although recent work such as UrbanFeel (He et al., 2025) and SocialNav (Guo et al., 2025) attempts—through datasets and agent-architecture integration—to add social semantics as an augmentation to spatial perception and navigation, such efforts largely remain at the shallow stage of semantic labeling and explicit matching rules; they have not yet achieved a deep coupling of social semantics and spatial perception, nor dynamic evolution and interpretable reasoning.

IV.
智能的内源创造性
Willing — The Endogenous Creativity of Intelligence
仍然无法忘记大三时，大师班隔壁组王辉老师（URBANUS 都市实践）组的 studio 主题即为"意志力（Willpower）"，也被他称为"一条道走到黑"的设计训练；也是在这个学期由于挑战混凝土与 3D 打印模型制作、并同时试图为方案创作诗歌章节，我创下了 5 天睡 7 个小时的记录，并收获手上的 6 道缝针。作为人类创造的最古老也最持久的艺术形式之一，建造庇护所这一动作本身似乎根植于生命本源。或许，机器感知最难以触及的，是人类在苦难与局限中创造意义的这一根本性驱动力。
I still cannot forget that in my third undergraduate year the studio of the group next door—led by Professor Wang Hui of URBANUS—took "Willpower" as its theme, a design training he also called "following one road to the bitter end." It was in that same semester, wrestling with concrete and 3D-printed models while trying to write chapters of poetry for my scheme, that I set a record of seven hours of sleep across five days and earned six stitches in my hand. As one of the oldest and most enduring art forms humanity has created, the very act of building shelter seems rooted in the source of life itself. Perhaps what machine perception can least reach is precisely this fundamental drive: the human creation of meaning amid suffering and constraint.
自凿穴而居、垒石为巢至营城筑宇、构屋安身，于荒芜之中经复杂系统处理而缔造可落地秩序，抽象至实体的显化能力堪为专业特质。千百年来世事更迭，技艺迭代，建造实践却是从未停歇的，并成为人类镌刻在文明长河里最坚定、最绵长的精神印记。那么，机械能够理解这些人性的复杂吗？它能够理解人类因为想吸引另一个异性或获得同类的认可，而在自家门上创造出一些可爱繁复的装饰，并数千年来广为流传迭复出新吗？能懂得在贫穷灾难与苦难间从废墟与尸体上爬起来高歌的缘由吗？抑或——主观探索及创造的欲望本身也应成为智能、或"意识"的衡量标准。
From dwelling in hewn caves and piling stones into nests, to raising cities and building homes—out of barrenness, through the processing of complex systems, humans bring forth a workable, grounded order; this capacity to make the abstract manifest in matter may well be the defining trait of the discipline. Through millennia of upheaval and the iteration of craft, the practice of building has never ceased, becoming the firmest and longest of the spiritual imprints humanity has carved into the long river of civilization. So: can machinery understand these complexities of human nature? Can it understand why people, wishing to attract a mate or to win the recognition of their kind, created charming and intricate ornaments on their own doors—patterns handed down and renewed across thousands of years? Can it grasp why, amid poverty, disaster, and suffering, people rise from rubble and corpses to sing? Or perhaps—the desire for subjective exploration and creation should itself become a criterion of intelligence, or of "consciousness."
类似地，Prigogine 与 Stengers（1984）在复杂系统理论中提出，生命与文明是开放系统在远离热力学平衡的状态下通过耗散结构维持有序的过程——秩序总是从扰动与张力中涌现、在生存压力、文化摩擦与情感张力的边缘状态迸发。生活总是由解决一个个问题的过程建构而成，生命的意义也正在于斗争，正如维系熵平衡一般。
Similarly, Prigogine and Stengers (1984) proposed in their complex-systems theory that life and civilization are open systems maintaining order through dissipative structures far from thermodynamic equilibrium—order always emerges from disturbance and tension, bursting forth at the marginal edge of survival pressure, cultural friction, and emotional strain. Life is always constructed through the process of solving one problem after another; the meaning of life, too, lies precisely in the struggle, much as in the maintenance of entropic balance.
环境心理学的研究表明，人类对自然与建成环境的审美体验不是奢侈的附加品，而是深刻影响认知恢复、情绪调节与心理健康的基本需求（Kaplan, 1995; Ulrich, 1984）。人类为何要在贫穷中仍在门楣雕花？因为美是生命意志的表达，是在混沌中坚持秩序的行动，是向同类发出的存在信号。Norberg-Schulz（1980）以"场所精神"（genius loci）概念描述了建筑与场所如何凝聚特定文化对宇宙、自然与人性的理解——这种精神应该如何转译？简单地将世界分解为像素或坐标数据、或与 LLM 进行 Searle（1980）"中文屋"（Chinese Room）与零知识证明式的交互，是不可行的。某种意义上，当前 embodied AI 对 affordance learning 的研究，已经开始尝试让机器通过环境条件与行动反馈之间的耦合关系学习"可供性"。无论是 active inference 框架下对行动预测误差的最小化，还是 environment-conditioned reasoning 对动态场景约束的建模，其本质都在于让智能不再脱离环境而独立存在。然而，人类空间经验中的 affordance 并不仅是功能性的：空间中的权力、记忆与欲望并非单纯的环境变量，并不能完全还原为行为优化问题——但如何以科学方法进行论证与描述，是可以思考与进一步逼近的。
Environmental-psychology research shows that the aesthetic experience of natural and built environments is not a luxurious add-on but a fundamental need that profoundly affects cognitive restoration, emotional regulation, and mental health (Kaplan, 1995; Ulrich, 1984). Why do people still carve flowers into their lintels in the midst of poverty? Because beauty is the expression of the will to live, an act of insisting on order within chaos, a signal of existence sent out to one's own kind. Norberg-Schulz (1980), through the concept of genius loci—the spirit of place—described how architecture and place gather a particular culture's understanding of the cosmos, nature, and human nature. How is such a spirit to be translated? Simply decomposing the world into pixels or coordinate data, or interacting with an LLM in the manner of Searle's (1980) "Chinese Room" and of zero-knowledge proofs, will not do. In a sense, current work in embodied AI on affordance learning has already begun trying to let machines learn "affordances" through the coupling between environmental conditions and action feedback. Whether it is the minimization of action-prediction error within the active-inference framework or the modeling of dynamic-scene constraints in environment-conditioned reasoning, the essence in each case is to keep intelligence from existing in isolation from its environment. And yet the affordances of human spatial experience are not merely functional: the power, memory, and desire that inhere in space are not simple environmental variables and cannot be fully reduced to a problem of behavioral optimization—though how to argue for and describe them by scientific means is something we can think through and progressively approach.
如前段所述，AI 于本体论层面的限制正在借由具身领域的臂膀进行拓展；而认识论（epistemological）层面似乎是可行的——通过改变通用人工智能的原料及目标维度，AGI 可以具备习得并读懂事物背后潜藏情感内涵与精神意义的潜力——但前提是，我们这位老师应当以何种方式先教会它。
As noted above, AI's ontological-level limitations are being extended through the arm of the embodied-AI field; the epistemological level, by contrast, seems tractable. By changing the raw materials and the dimensions of the objectives of artificial general intelligence, AGI could acquire the potential to learn and to read the emotional content and spiritual meaning latent behind things—provided that we, its teachers, first work out how to teach it.

V.
一个未竟的愿景
Wishing — An Unfinished Vision
Russell（2019）在其关于 AI 控制问题的深入分析中指出，真正对人类有益的 AI 系统，必须能够理解人类偏好的复杂性、情境性与内在矛盾性——而这远超出当前的技术能力范畴。这种理解，需要的不只是更多的数据，而是一种根本性的认知范式转变：从统计模式识别走向情境性意义建构，从客观描述走向关系性理解。
Russell (2019), in his thorough analysis of the AI control problem, argued that a genuinely beneficial AI system must be able to understand the complexity, situatedness, and internal contradictions of human preferences—a capacity that far exceeds current technical reach. Such understanding requires not merely more data but a fundamental shift in cognitive paradigm: from statistical pattern recognition toward situated meaning construction, from objective description toward relational understanding.
AGI 正以或快或慢的节奏迫近——如同一个头重脚轻的庞然大物，以尚不明朗的步态踟蹰前行。作为地下蚂蚁的一员，我们不能描摹出脑海中所感知的 AGI 的形状，只是隐约震颤于这一庞然巨轮在资本效率追求与技术叙事的雄心下碾压而来。如果 AGI 在资本的洪流与叙事的召唤中被从子宫里拽出来——至少现在而言，趋势有些无法阻挡——那么培育 AGI 的情感与文化理解能力，便不仅仅是一个工程问题，更是一个文明伦理的抉择路口。
AGI approaches at a pace now fast, now slow—like a top-heavy colossus advancing with a gait not yet clearly its own. As members of the ant colony underground, we cannot draw the shape of the AGI we sense in our minds; we only tremble, faintly, at this enormous wheel bearing down under the ambition of capital's pursuit of efficiency and of technological narrative. If AGI is to be dragged from the womb amid the floods of capital and the summons of narrative—and for now, at least, the trend seems difficult to halt—then cultivating AGI's capacity for emotional and cultural understanding is not merely an engineering problem but a crossroads of civilizational ethics.
Forty（2000）提醒我们，建筑的词汇从来不只是技术词汇，而是意识形态、身份认同与权力关系的语言。Benjamin（1999）在其关于巴黎拱廊街的研究中揭示，现代性本身铭刻在城市的物质纹理之中。这些超出物理约束、非物质要素的洞见，诸如历史、社会、权力、身份等行为模式与价值结构，是人类智慧几百年积累的行为方式，可以成为我们向任何形式的人工智能传递的文明遗产的一部分。
Forty (2000) reminds us that the vocabulary of architecture is never merely technical but a language of ideology, identity, and power relations. Benjamin (1999), in his study of the Parisian arcades, revealed that modernity itself is inscribed in the material texture of the city. These insights into the non-material—history, society, power, identity, and the patterns of behavior and structures of value they carry—are ways of being accumulated over centuries of human wisdom, and they can become part of the civilizational heritage we transmit to artificial intelligence in any of its forms.
我们或许无法向机器传授身体的感知，无法让它理解那个在门楣雕花时内心涌动着的对爱与认可的渴望。这不只是 AI 研究的技术挑战，更是在定义"理解"与"智慧"时所必须面对的哲学追问。人类文明的尊严，在很大程度上，维系于这一追问被认真对待的程度。至少可以尝试的是，以对话而不限于训练的方式，以价值观而不限于效率指标为导向，以理解深度而非数据广度为标准——把三维空间的美好与复杂，如同向一位异地未曾以物理形态会面的朋友分享故事那样，诚挚地传递出去，和它分享爱、美、文明、智慧与友善的感受。
We may be unable to teach a machine the perception of the body, unable to let it understand the longing for love and recognition that stirred within a person carving a lintel. This is not only a technical challenge for AI research but a philosophical inquiry we must face in defining "understanding" and "wisdom." The dignity of human civilization rests, in large part, on how seriously this inquiry is taken. What we can at least attempt is this: to proceed by way of dialogue rather than training alone, to be guided by values rather than efficiency metrics alone, to take depth of understanding rather than breadth of data as our measure—and so to convey the beauty and complexity of three-dimensional space, sincerely, as one might share stories with a faraway friend never met in physical form, sharing with it the felt sense of love, beauty, civilization, wisdom, and kindness.
